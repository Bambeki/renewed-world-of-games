## What the tests establish

Campaign planners issue the same legal movement, interaction, recruitment, attack and turn actions available to a player. Browser tests execute those actions through the actual interface, including all mission transitions and final replay. They do not load fabricated victory saves. Engine tests also cover defeat, retry, role restrictions, blocked movement, attack/recruitment limits, immutability and invalid save payloads.

Browser testing found and fixed a Roman-interface defect: when the selected unit died, a surviving unit could not be selected by clicking. A dedicated regression now kills the selected unit and issues a move through a surviving unit. An adventure regression verifies that reaching the exit with incomplete objectives cannot bypass storm defeat.

The football drill shares the existing fixed-step physics and rendering. Its new regression verifies that only the attacker/keeper are active, hidden players remain immobile, and passes cannot target hidden teammates. Existing full-exhibition tests remain in place.

## Limits of this evidence

- No claim of physical Safari/iPhone/Android/controller verification. Touch and gamepad browser automation are emulations.
- No usability, enjoyment, retention, full accessibility or low-end-device frame-time study has been performed.
- Local PGlite checks do not replace native PostgreSQL concurrency/migration testing. CI retains the API integration suite and every existing browser suite for that purpose.
- The 3D football experience remains experimental. The new campaigns are short local prototypes, not commercial content-complete releases.
- Production deployment, backup restoration, account/session hardening, multiplayer and payments are not verified by these checks.
