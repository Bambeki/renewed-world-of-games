# Renewed World of Games

## Vision

Renewed World of Games is a multiplayer gaming platform that connects people around the world through interactive games and social experiences.

The goal is to make online gaming feel more like being together in the same room, even when players are in different countries.

## MVP (Version 1)

The first version will focus on a football management game.

Features:

* User registration and login
* Create and manage football teams
* Build and train players
* Match simulation
* League and championship system
* Transfer market
* Global multiplayer competition
* Text chat between players

## Future Social Features

* Voice chat
* Video chat during matches
* Spectator mode
* Fan communities
* Friend groups and clubs

All social features will be optional so users can choose the experience they prefer.

## Future AI Features

* AI football coach
* AI scouting assistant
* AI match analysis
* AI-generated tournaments
* AI-generated players and storylines

AI features will be optional and may be offered as premium features.

## Future Expansion

After the football game is successful, the platform may expand to include:

* Car racing games
* Card games
* Additional multiplayer experiences

## Mission

To create a new generation of online games where players can compete, communicate, and build communities regardless of their location.
